# Competitor Analysis — Stacked Construction
**Market:** Southwest Florida Home Remodeling | **Updated:** April 2026

---

## Competitive Landscape Summary

The SWFL remodeling market has fragmented competition — no single dominant player owns the full service-area matrix. Most competitors serve 1–3 cities and lack comprehensive blog content. Stacked Construction's 6-city coverage with 40+ blog articles is already a structural advantage.

## Strategic Recommendations Based on Competition

1. **Become the Houzz leader in SWFL** — every competitor has a Houzz profile but most are sparse. A fully built-out Houzz profile with 50+ project photos, 30+ reviews, and regular posts could make Stacked Construction the #1 Houzz result for multiple SWFL searches.

2. **Blog moat** — Curtis Allen Designs (40+ years, 9 cities) has zero blog. Creating 2 posts/week targeting their geographic footprint captures their organic traffic without competing on domain age.

3. **Lead magnet gap** — only Cornerstone offers a PDF lead magnet. A "Naples Kitchen Remodel Planning Guide" PDF captures emails and builds trust that no other competitor offers at this level.

4. **YouTube first-mover** — Curtis Allen Designs and Reed don't have YouTube. KGT and Cornerstone do but appear under-invested. Being first with quality project timelapse videos creates a moat that's hard to replicate quickly.
