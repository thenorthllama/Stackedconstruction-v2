# SEO Strategy — Stacked Construction
**Domain:** stackedconstruction.co | **Updated:** April 2026

---

## Business Context

Stacked Construction is a Southwest Florida home remodeling contractor (FL License #CBC1269972) serving Lee, Collier, and Charlotte counties. The site is a Next.js 16 SSG site with ~84 pre-generated pages, 40+ blog articles, and a GEO score of 71/100. The goal is to rank for high-intent local remodeling queries across 6 cities and convert visitors into consultation requests.

**Primary conversion:** Phone call to (239) 785-0166 or contact form submission  
**Target audience:** Homeowners aged 35–65 in SWFL with $50K–$300K+ remodeling budgets

## Strategic Pillars

### Pillar 1 — Local Authority Dominance
Own the top 3 Google positions for `[service] + [city]` across all 30 city–service combinations. Expand to neighborhood-level targeting within Naples and Fort Myers.

### Pillar 2 — Content Leadership
Be the most comprehensive remodeling resource in SWFL. Target informational queries that competitors ignore (design guides, cost comparisons, permit guides, HOA remodeling rules).

### Pillar 3 — Off-Site Brand Presence
Eliminate the #1 AI citation gap: build Houzz, Angi, Google Business Profile, and YouTube presence so ChatGPT and Perplexity can cite Stacked Construction in local recommendations.

### Pillar 4 — Conversion Rate Optimization
Every organic visit should have a clear, low-friction path to a consultation. Optimize CTAs, add social proof, and build trust signals at key decision points.

## Priority Quick Wins (Do This Month)

1. **FAQPage schema** — Add to all 5 service pages. Low effort, direct Google rich result.
2. **Google Business Profile** — Verify, complete all fields, post weekly. #1 local ranking factor.
3. **Houzz listing** — Create profile, upload 20 project photos. ChatGPT pulls ~47% of contractor citations from Houzz.
4. **Author bio pages** — Create `/about/junior-da-silva`, `/about/camila-lage`, `/about/caio-alves` with credentials and link them from blog posts.
5. **Reviews page** — Build `/reviews` page pulling from Google or embedding testimonials with ReviewSchema.
