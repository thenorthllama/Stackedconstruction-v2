## Content Quality Standards

- **Minimum 1,200 words** per blog post (competitor average is 800)
- **1 original comparison table** per post where applicable
- **Local specificity**: every post must mention at least 2 SWFL cities, neighborhoods, or landmarks
- **Internal links**: minimum 3 internal links per post to service pages or city pages
- **Author byline**: every post linked to author bio page
- **Target featured snippet**: include an H2 answering the primary question in 40–60 words at the top

---

## E-E-A-T Content Priorities

To build **Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trust** signals:

1. **Project case studies** — Before/after with specific costs, timeline, materials, and client city
2. **Author credential pages** — License numbers, years of experience, service area expertise
3. **"Why We Do It This Way"** posts — Explaining construction decisions (builds trust with AI crawlers)
4. **Local award mentions** — If any awards exist, create a dedicated section or page
5. **External citations** — Link to NAHB, NKBA, local building codes in relevant posts
